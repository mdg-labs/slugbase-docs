## What an organization is

On Cloud, bookmarks and related data are scoped to an **organization** (your billing and plan attach to that organization). Self-hosted SlugBase is single-tenant; Cloud adds this layer so many customers can share one deployment safely.

## Your first workspace

When you sign up, SlugBase automatically creates an organization for you on the **Free** plan and makes you the **owner**. This becomes your default workspace. If you're later invited to another organization, you can switch between them in the app.

## Members and roles

Each organization has **members** with a **role** (listed with join time and basic profile fields for people you are allowed to see). **Owners** and **admins** can invite users on eligible plans (see [Collaboration](/cloud/collaboration)).

## Invitations (Team plan)

Creating invitations is enforced server-side: only **owner** or **admin** may invite, and the organization must be on the **Team** plan. If the plan is not Team, you see an error indicating you need to upgrade to invite members. Seat limits apply when adding people (see [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans-and-limits) and [Billing](/cloud/billing)).

## Accepting an organization invitation

You must be **signed in** before acceptance can succeed. The address on your account must **match the email** the invitation was sent to—otherwise acceptance is blocked. When you complete the flow, the server checks the invitation token you were given (for example from the email or the page that opened after you followed a link).

There is no separate product URL path documented in the open app for org invites; follow the **link or instructions in the invitation email** (or from whoever invited you). Create an account or **sign in using that same email**, then finish acceptance as the app or email directs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Use the invited email" icon="mail" titleType="p">
    Open the invitation link or follow instructions from the inviter. If you need an account, sign up with the **same email** the invitation was sent to. If you already have an account, sign in with that email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay signed in" icon="log-in" titleType="p">
    Complete acceptance while logged in as that user. If you are on the wrong account or a different email, switch accounts or fix the email first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete acceptance" icon="check-circle" titleType="p">
    Finish the steps the product shows after you follow the link. The server validates your token and adds you to the organization when everything matches.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If something goes wrong, typical cases include: **invitation not found**, **already used** (or cancelled), **expired**, **wrong email** (the invite was sent to a different address than your signed-in account), or **no spare seats** on the Team plan—in that case, ask an **owner** or **admin** to add seats in [Billing](/cloud/billing), then try again.

## Multiple organizations

You can belong to more than one organization — for example your personal workspace and a team workspace. Plan and billing are managed separately per organization.
