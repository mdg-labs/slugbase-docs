## Cookie preferences

The Cloud site shows **Cookie preferences** (`cookieConsent` copy). In summary:

- **Strictly necessary** cookies are always on (session, authentication, security).
- If you opt in to **Analytics**, the product may load **Umami** (usage analytics) and **Sentry** (error reporting) as described in the consent UI.
- **Functional** and **Marketing** categories are described as available for future use; you can change choices anytime (**Manage preferences**).

For how personal data is processed when you use the hosted service, rely on the [Privacy Policy](https://slugbase.app/privacy).
