## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you can update general workspace settings, enable or disable AI suggestions for your workspace, and understand which configuration is operator-managed on CE instances.

## Prerequisites

- Signed in as a workspace **owner** or **admin**
- Active workspace selected

## Open workspace settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    From the sidebar, select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Workspace">
    Under **Workspace**, choose **General** or another section from the workspace settings navigation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Members without admin rights see a notice that workspace settings are restricted to admins and owners.

## General settings

On the **General** section, update the **Workspace name** and click **Save changes**. The name appears in the workspace switcher and member-facing screens.

## Workspace settings sections (CE)

On a typical CE instance, workspace admins see these sections under **Workspace**:

| Section | What admins can change | Operator-managed? |
|---------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **General** | Workspace name and deletion | No |
| **Email / SMTP** | Read-only notice only | Yes — see [SMTP](/ce/smtp) |
| **AI suggestions** | Enable/disable toggle | Credential is operator-managed — see [AI suggestions](/ce/ai-suggestions) |
| **Identity providers** | Read-only notice only | Yes — see [OIDC](/ce/oidc) |

SMTP transport, OIDC provider definitions, and AI API credentials are configured exclusively by the instance operator through deployment environment variables. Workspace admins do not enter passwords, client secrets, or API keys in the UI.

The **Billing** group does not appear on CE instances.

## Enable AI suggestions

Workspace admins control whether AI suggestions are available in their workspace:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI suggestions">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace → AI suggestions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle and save">
    Turn **Enable AI suggestions** on or off and click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The operator must configure `OPENAI_API_KEY` on the deployment before suggestions can work. See [AI suggestions](/ce/ai-suggestions) for the full operator and admin workflow.

## Delete a workspace

<Callout kind="danger" title="Important">
Deleting a workspace permanently removes all bookmarks, folders, tags, teams, and settings in that workspace. This cannot be undone.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General settings">
    Scroll to **Delete workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start deletion">
    Click **Delete workspace…** and type the workspace name to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm permanently">
    Click **Delete permanently**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only workspace owners can delete a workspace. At least one owner must remain until deletion is confirmed.

## Verify it worked

After saving general settings, the updated workspace name appears in the sidebar switcher. AI toggle changes show a success toast when saved.
