## What this is for

You can **permanently delete your account** from the **Profile** page. That removes your user record and associated data from this SlugBase instance (subject to how the server and database enforce cascades). This action is **irreversible** from the product’s perspective—you should [export](/selfhosted/import-export) anything you need first.

## Before you start

- You must be signed in.
- Some deployments attach an extra check before the delete dialog. If deletion is blocked, you may see **You cannot delete your account. Transfer ownership or resolve billing first.** or another message from the operator. Stock self-hosted core does not show that guard unless the deployment wires one in.

<Callout kind="danger">
  The in-app **Danger zone** copy warns: **Permanently delete your account and all your data. This action cannot be undone.** The confirmation dialog adds: **Are you sure you want to delete your account? All your bookmarks, folders, tags, and data will be permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.**
</Callout>

## Delete your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile" icon="user" titleType="p">
    From the top bar user menu, choose **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Danger zone" icon="alert-triangle" titleType="p">
    Scroll to **Danger zone**. The section description is **Irreversible actions. These cannot be undone.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start deletion" icon="user-x" titleType="p">
    Select **Delete my account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm" icon="check" titleType="p">
    In the confirmation dialog, confirm again. The primary action uses the same label **Delete my account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After a successful deletion, the app shows **Your account has been deleted.**, signs you out, and sends you to the login screen.

## What happens on the server

The client calls **`DELETE /api/users/me`** while you are authenticated. The server deletes your user row and clears the auth cookie in the response so the browser session ends. Related rows (for example API tokens, team memberships, and other data tied to your user) are removed according to the database’s foreign-key rules for that installation.

<Callout kind="info">
  Exact retention or backups depend on **your** hosting and database practices. The app does not replace your own backup or recovery policy.
</Callout>

## If you cannot delete

- **Blocked by policy:** If the message mentions ownership or billing, follow what it says or ask your organization admin.
- **Other errors:** Read the toast or error text; retry after fixing the stated problem or contact your instance administrator.
